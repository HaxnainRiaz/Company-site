# Homepage and service workspace redesign

This pass follows the mobile product walkthrough as the interaction reference. It replaces repeated capability accordions and pale section bands, and rebuilds the homepage.

## Homepage

- A playable hero with three distinct scenes: storefront basket, enquiry handover, and mobile booking.
- A service explorer with compact, relevant original artwork and direct service links.
- An editorial gallery linking to working commerce, appointment, and operations demos.
- A continuous workflow preview, interactive project-process scenes, contextual photography, studio notes, FAQs, and a new invitation to contact.
- No fabricated outcome metrics or testimonials were added.

## Service detail pages

- Eight purpose-specific capability canvases: website workshop, commerce engine, app release board, conversation routing, connected workflow, retail desk, search inspection, and campaign editor.
- All existing subservices remain available through the capability chapters and detail selectors.
- The former design-breakdown and integration accordions are replaced by an interactive systems section. Each service has an appropriate diagram treatment and selectable connection requirements.
- The mobile product walkthrough remains intact.
- Large pale experience, capability, system, and FAQ bands use the footer's `#0b0d1b` background, with violet accents and white product surfaces.

## Motion and assets

- A single elapsed-time source drives the workflow path, travelling record, node states, and receipt. Pausing preserves its position; offscreen, hidden-tab, global pause, and reduced-motion states stop progression.
- Mobile uses a continuous vertical route with branches into each node.
- Existing optimized local photography and original WebP illustrations are combined with code-built, responsive product scenes. No GIF or animation library download was added.
- Homepage service data is reduced to the fields needed by the interactive explorer.

## Manual checks during development

- Homepage basket, enquiry assignment, and booking interactions.
- SEO chapter selection, feature detail selection, and integration inspection.
- Campaign editor chapter selection.
- Workflow exception route and pause control.
- Phone-width screenshots identified and resolved a clipped hero phone and missing word spacing in the large statement.
- A hot-reload icon mismatch was observed; the final verification uses a fresh production build rather than relying on that development session.
